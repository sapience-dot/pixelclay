Before you ask

Common questions.

Do you work with clients outside Davanagere?

Yes. Most of the work happens over calls and shared files, and we travel within Karnataka for research and press checks.

Can you design in Kannada as well as English?

Yes, and we do it by default for anything local. Logos, signage, menus and websites get both scripts set with equal care.

Who owns the final files?

You do. Every final file, editable source and guideline is handed over when the project ends.

How do payments work?

You get a written quote with a fixed fee. It is usually split into a payment to start and a payment at handover, with GST shown separately.

What if I only have a rough idea?

That is the best place to start. The first call is free, and we will tell you plainly which service fits, or if you need none of them yet.

About

A studio in Davanagere.

Good design should not need a move to Bengaluru. So we built the studio at home.

A pixel is the smallest exact unit on a screen. Clay is the oldest material people shape by hand. Our work sits between the two: strict grids and systems, finished by hand until they feel right.

We stay small on purpose. The people at your first meeting are the people who design your project, and you reach us directly on the phone or WhatsApp.
