FAQ

Questions.

Where are you based?

Bengaluru, India. We work with clients across Asia, Europe, the Middle East and North America.

Do you produce video end to end?

Yes. Script, storyboard, shoot, edit, sound and grade, or animation from first sketch to final render.

How do you handle time zones?

We keep a few overlap hours for calls and work async the rest of the day.
