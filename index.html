The name

A pixel is the smallest exact unit on a screen. Clay is the oldest material people shape by hand. Our work sits between the two: strict grids and systems, finished by hand until they feel right.

We stay small on purpose. The people at your first meeting are the people who design your project, and you reach us directly on the phone or WhatsApp.

How we work

Three things we hold to.

Local first

We design for the street the shop stands on: bilingual by default, readable in bright sun, printable at the press down the road.

Fixed and fair

A written scope and a fixed fee before we start. If the scope changes, we quote the change first.

Yours to keep

You own every final file. Guidelines and editable sources come with the handover at no extra cost.
